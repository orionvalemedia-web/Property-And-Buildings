# Property & Buildings

All 6 products in this package.

Every entry below is taken from the package's product inventory. Nothing here is a plan or a
roadmap item; all of it is built.

---

## Devadex StorageOS

144 files, 25 tests

Operations platform for independent self-storage: autopay billing, tenant ledger with 30/60/90 ageing, delinquency staging, tenant protection revenue, and a coded lien and auction workflow.

## Devadex Property Manager OS

90 files, 38 tests + 5 end-to-end

Full property-management suite: leasing, rent ledger, maintenance, work orders, vendors, inspections, accounting and profit-and-loss.

## Devadex Real Estate OS

95 files, 35 tests + 2 end-to-end

Agent-facing platform: properties, listings, a drag-and-drop transaction pipeline with commission tracking, contacts, marketing and reporting.

## ADA Retrofit Planner

8 files, 81 tests

Performs an accessibility audit of a commercial property and generates a prioritised remediation plan, ranked by legal exposure so the owner fixes the actionable items first.

## Property Tax Appealer

15 files, 75 tests

Takes an assessment notice and property details and builds the appeal.

## Cost Seg Engine

14 files, 66 tests

Produces an engineering-style cost segregation study so a property owner can accelerate depreciation and pull deductions forward.
